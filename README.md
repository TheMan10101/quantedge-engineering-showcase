# QuantEdge
### Python Data Engineering | Time-Series Analytics | AI-Assisted Engineering

**Independent Engineering & Research Project**

QuantEdge is an independently developed Python-based research and analytics system focused on processing historical time-series data, validating analytical results, and building repeatable engineering workflows.

The project combines data engineering, statistical analysis, automated testing, and AI-assisted software development, with an emphasis on reliability, reproducibility, and human oversight.

## Engineering Highlights

**Data Engineering & Analytics**
- Directed and reviewed development of Python analytics workflows processing more than 250,000 historical time-series records.
- Developed repeatable workflows for data ingestion, cleaning, transformation, and validation using Python and pandas.
- Applied statistical analysis and structured performance measurements to historical research datasets.
- Established data-quality checks, anomaly controls, and traceable analytical outputs.

**Software Engineering & Testing**
- Guided AI-assisted development and testing of Python-based research components.
- Applied automated validation, regression testing, and structured engineering reviews.
- Used Git version control, documentation, and controlled development workflows.
- Emphasized reproducibility, error detection, and separation of research from live execution.

**Reporting & Visualization**
- Developed Streamlit-based reporting workflows and dashboards.
- Organized research metrics, data-quality indicators, and operational status reporting.
- Used structured CSV datasets and analytical reporting to support research decisions.
